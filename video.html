Editing Specialization

Long-Form Craftsmanship

Pacing a 20-minute video requires a completely different mindset than quick clips. Here is how I approach long-form visual architecture.

🎥

Narrative & Video Essays

Structuring deep-dive videos with compelling narrative arcs. Balancing pacing so viewers stay engaged through complex explanations without mental fatigue.

  • Story tension & retention architecture
  • Elimination of awkward pauses & dead air
  • Intentional breathers and pacing peaks
🎨

Color Grading & Mood

Transforming flat log footage into deep, moody, film-grade imagery in DaVinci Resolve. Perfecting skin tones while giving the project a cohesive cinematic tone.

  • Log to Rec.709 color transformation
  • Skin-tone isolation & consistency
  • Custom film curve emulation & grain
🎧

Immersive Soundscapes

Sound is 50% of the viewing experience. I layer foley, room atmospheres, whooshes, and music ducking so audio feels alive, crisp, and intentional.

  • Dialogue isolation & frequency cleanup
  • Tactile foley & transition impacts
  • Dynamic audio ducking under speech
🎞️

Visual Graphics & B-Roll

Clean, restrained motion design that clarifies ideas rather than distracting from them. Highlighting documents, maps, code snippets, and UI mockups.

  • Cinematic B-roll framing & zooms
  • Document highlight & paper textures
  • Subtle kinetic lower-thirds & diagrams
